## "Why switch now?"

> "I have got 8 years in telecom and I feel I have squeezed the most out of this domain. I am at a point where I want a bigger challenge, better growth and honestly better compensation too. So this feels like the right time."

---

## "Why so soon? Only 1 year at current company?"

> "Fair question — but my overall journey is 8 years, not 1. I joined this role expecting certain growth but things did not shape up the way I anticipated. I am someone who would rather act early than wait and regret later."

---

## "Are you being laid off?"

> "Not at all. I put in my resignation myself. Completely voluntary. I have had a stable 8 year run and this is purely my own decision for career growth."

---

## "Moving out of telecom — won't you go back?"

> "Telecom gave me a strong engineering foundation — distributed systems, cloud-native Java, large scale backend work. These skills travel well across domains. I am genuinely looking to apply them in a broader context now and I am not looking back."

---

## "What is your expected salary?"

> "Based on my experience and what the market looks like, I am targeting somewhere around X to Y. But I am open to talking through the full package — there is some flexibility depending on the role and growth potential."

---

## "Where do you see yourself in 5 years?"

> "Honestly, I want to be someone who is solving hard engineering problems at scale and helping the team around me grow. Whether that is a senior engineer or architect role, I am open — I care more about impact than the title."

---

## "Why this company specifically?"

> "I did my research and the kind of engineering problems you are solving genuinely interest me. The scale and the tech stack align well with where I want to go. It does not feel like just another job switch — it feels like the right next step."

---

## "What is your notice period?"

> "Three months currently. But depending on how the handover goes, there might be room to negotiate an earlier release."

---

## "Why should we hire you?"

> "8 years of solid backend engineering in a complex domain, strong fundamentals, and someone who takes ownership and delivers. I am not here just for a job change — I am genuinely looking to contribute and grow. That combination is what I bring."

## "HR says no budget for your expected salary"

> "Got it, I appreciate you being upfront. What range do you have in mind? Let me see if we can find a middle ground."

If their number is too low:

> "I hear you on the budget. Is there any room to work with on joining bonus or a faster appraisal cycle? I really like the role and I want to make this work if possible."

If still too rigid:

> "I understand. I do like the opportunity but I also need the move to make financial sense for me. Let me think it over and come back to you."
